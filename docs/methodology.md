# Methodology

Phase 1 establishes a research framework. There are no property findings, business presets or financial results yet.

## Reasoning chain

Physical asset → evidenced constraints → possible business concepts → explicit financial assumptions → calculated results.

A sanatorium is an asset, not a nursing-home designation. Concepts can combine service models and address different target markets independently of the property record. Independent living, assisted living / Betreuung, long-term nursing / Pflege, dementia care, rehabilitation and health resort remain distinct identifiers.

Important factual values will display their effective date, source and verification status. Source category and verification status are independent. Missing facts remain unknown. See [research-standard.md](research-standard.md).

## Sequence

1. Skeleton and local technical checks.
2. Property inventory and evidence research.
3. Business concepts, explicit financial assumptions and model.
4. Dossiers, comparison, interactive map and calculator UI.
5. Substantive translations and editorial/native Georgian review.
6. Full data, financial, accessibility and visual validation.
7. Preview verification and then production deployment.

Technical checks continue in each phase. Public deployment follows completed content and validation.
