# Research workspace

This directory is intentionally empty of substantive research in Phase 1.

- `properties/`: individual asset dossiers and working notes.
- `market/`: customer-market, pricing and operator evidence.
- `legal/`: registry, ownership and regulatory research.
- `sources/`: retained documents where storage and reuse are permitted.

Follow [the research standard](../docs/research-standard.md). Reviewed records belong in the typed collections under `src/data/`. Working notes do not automatically become published facts. Source links and document locators can be recorded without copying protected documents.

Use the [Concept & Operating Model Matrix](../docs/strategy/concept-operating-model-matrix.md) to tag findings and property suitability with relevant `TSK-S1` through `TSK-S7` scenario IDs and service-building-block IDs. Keep concept definitions and decision statuses in that document.
